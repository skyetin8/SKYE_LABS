# Skye Labs Vision

SKYE_LABS — WEBSITE DESIGN & DEVELOPMENT PROMPT

Create a high-end, modern, minimal website for SKYE_LABS, an independent Black-owned product design and technology company based in New York City.

The website should feel like a serious emerging design/technology company with a strong point of view—not like a generic startup template, ecommerce store, nonprofit website, or personal portfolio.

The visual identity should communicate:

PRECISION. POSSIBILITY. INTELLIGENCE. RESTRAINT. HUMANITY. EXPERIMENTATION.

The company is currently in its earliest stage, so the website should feel intentionally designed and established without falsely implying that every future initiative is already operational.

1. BRAND

Primary brand name:

SKYE_LABS

Parent organization / future organizational architecture:

SKYE_GROUP

For now, SKYE_LABS is the active public-facing brand.

Where appropriate, the brand may be presented as:

SKYE_LABS
by SKYE_GROUP

However, do not make SKYE_GROUP the dominant brand. SKYE_LABS should be the primary identity.

Temporary tagline:

NEVER IMPOSSIBLE.

Temporary mission statement:

SKYE_LABS IS A BLACK-OWNED PRODUCT DESIGN COMPANY.

WE SEEK TO INTERRUPT COMMUNITY DIVISION AND ISOLATION THROUGH EXCELLENT PRODUCT DESIGN.

This language is provisional and will be replaced/edited later. Build the site so all major copy can easily be changed without redesigning the interface.

2. ABSOLUTE VISUAL RULES

The visual system MUST use exclusively:

BLACK + WHITE

No other colors.

Do not introduce:

gray gradients

blue

beige

cream

green

purple

orange

accent colors

colorful illustrations

colorful buttons

gradients

Black and white may be used in different tonal relationships through opacity, borders, shadows, spacing, photography treatment, and typography, but the overall visual palette must remain strictly monochromatic.

Typography

Use INTER for EVERYTHING.

Do not use another font.

Use different weights, sizes, tracking, line heights, and capitalization to establish hierarchy.

Typography should feel editorial, architectural, contemporary, and extremely intentional.

Use:

ALL CAPS for major navigation, section headers, labels, buttons, and other intentional interface elements.

SKYE_LABS exactly as written for the brand name.

Sentence case / normal capitalization for body copy and longer explanatory text.

Do not make entire paragraphs uppercase.

Avoid decorative typography.

Avoid serif fonts.

Avoid futuristic novelty fonts.

Avoid excessive font weights.

The typography itself should carry much of the design.

3. OVERALL DESIGN LANGUAGE

Think:

Contemporary industrial design studio + technology laboratory + editorial publication + emerging cultural institution.

The site should feel sophisticated enough that someone could encounter SKYE_LABS for the first time and believe:

“This company has a very clear point of view.”

Do NOT make it feel:

corporate

SaaS-like

childish

overly futuristic

crypto-like

Web3-like

overly “AI startup”

nonprofit-template-like

overly luxurious

fashion-brand-like

cluttered

Use restraint.

Whitespace is extremely important.

Large typography, strong alignment, deliberate spacing, thin borders, monochrome imagery, and asymmetrical but controlled layouts are encouraged.

Avoid excessive rounded cards.

Avoid excessive pill-shaped UI.

Avoid excessive shadows.

Avoid glassmorphism.

Avoid animated gradients.

Avoid unnecessary decorative elements.

The site should feel expensive because it is restrained, not because it contains visual effects everywhere.

4. FIGMA LAYOUT

I have an existing Figma layout that establishes the intended information architecture and visual direction.

Treat the Figma design as the primary structural reference.

Preserve the major relationships, hierarchy, rhythm, and intended sections of the Figma design rather than replacing it with a generic website template.

Where the Figma design is incomplete, use the design principles in this prompt to complete it.

The site should be responsive and maintain the same design philosophy across desktop, tablet, and mobile.

Do not simply shrink the desktop site for mobile.

5. PRIMARY WEBSITE NAVIGATION

The primary public navigation should contain exactly three major destinations:

VISION
PRODUCTS
JOIN

The SKYE_LABS logo/wordmark should return to the homepage.

Keep navigation minimal.

The navigation should feel almost architectural.

Potential desktop structure:

SKYE_LABS VISION PRODUCTS JOIN

Do not add unnecessary navigation items.

Secondary pages can exist beneath these major sections, but they should not clutter the primary navigation.

6. HOMEPAGE

The homepage should introduce SKYE_LABS to someone who knows absolutely nothing about the company.

The homepage should answer three questions quickly:

WHAT IS SKYE_LABS?

WHAT ARE WE MAKING?

WHY SHOULD I CARE / HOW CAN I PARTICIPATE?

The homepage should prominently introduce:

SKYE_LABS

NEVER IMPOSSIBLE.

Then introduce the company through the three central ideas:

VISION
PRODUCTS
JOIN

The homepage should not attempt to explain every future initiative in detail.

It should create curiosity and establish the company's worldview.

7. VISION

VISION should be a substantial, immersive section/page.

This is where SKYE_LABS explains what it is ultimately trying to build.

The Vision page should be significantly more expansive than the homepage.

Structure the page around three stages:

PHASE 1

PRODUCTS

SKYE_LABS begins with small physical products and inventions designed for everyday life.

The first products should demonstrate the company's ability to identify ordinary problems, develop useful solutions, manufacture physical objects, and bring them directly to consumers.

Products may initially launch through:

direct-to-consumer sales

Kickstarter campaigns

other appropriate crowdfunding/preorder mechanisms

eventually larger distribution channels

The profits from successful products will help fund subsequent technological and social initiatives.

The website should make clear that Phase 1 is the immediate focus.

PHASE 2

TECHNOLOGY

Phase 2 expands the company beyond physical products into software.

The long-term vision currently includes two major applications.

APP CONCEPT 01 — COMMUNITY FUNDING

A faith-oriented community funding platform designed to make it easier for people to support churches and potentially other community institutions directly.

The broader concept is similar in spirit to crowdfunding/payment infrastructure, but with an emphasis on community financial participation.

The eventual platform may allow churches and other eligible organizations to receive contributions through a simple, accessible system.

The business model, transaction structure, compliance requirements, and nonprofit relationships are still being developed.

Do NOT represent this as an existing operational financial service.

APP CONCEPT 02 — COMMUNITY CONNECTION

A platform designed to help people form meaningful friendships and local communities.

The concept is to match people into small cohorts of potential friends based on geography and other relevant compatibility factors.

The larger goal is to address isolation and loneliness in an increasingly technology-dependent world by using technology to facilitate real-world human connection.

The language should emphasize that the technology is intended to help people get OFF the screen and INTO community.

8. PHASE 3

COMMUNITY

Phase 3 represents the longer-term social vision of SKYE_LABS / SKYE_GROUP.

Potential initiatives include:

community stabilization

support for people experiencing homelessness

transitional/restabilization housing

community financial resilience

investment in struggling community institutions

partnerships with churches and community organizations

shelters and supportive housing

potentially international initiatives

This phase should feel aspirational rather than falsely operational.

Use language such as:

“Our long-term vision…”

“We hope to build…”

“Over time…”

Do not claim that SKYE_LABS currently operates shelters, nonprofits, financial platforms, or international programs unless explicitly provided in future content.

9. THE PROBLEM

Create a deeper page/section accessible from the VISION experience but NOT listed as a primary homepage navigation item.

The purpose of THE PROBLEM is to explain the human problems that motivate SKYE_LABS.

Potential areas include:

ISOLATION

The increasing difficulty of forming meaningful relationships in a technologically connected but socially fragmented world.

HOMELESSNESS

The dangers and instability associated with homelessness, and the need for systems that help people move toward genuine stability rather than simply survive crisis-to-crisis.

FINANCIAL INSECURITY

The fragility of individuals and communities when people lack access to financial support networks.

Explore the idea that communities can become more resilient when people have mechanisms for helping one another.

COMMUNITY DISCONNECTION

The broader fragmentation of communities and institutions.

The page should communicate that SKYE_LABS is interested in designing practical interventions rather than merely commenting on social problems.

The writing will be supplied and refined later.

Build the page architecture so additional issues can easily be added.

10. PRIVATE / FUTURE INITIATIVES

Do not currently make the faith-based LGBTQ / church-hurt initiative a prominent public section.

This is currently private and should not be exposed through the primary navigation.

The website architecture should nevertheless be flexible enough that additional initiatives can be introduced later.

Do not invent details about this initiative.

11. PRODUCTS

PRODUCTS should eventually become the ecommerce-facing portion of the site.

For now, create a sophisticated product catalog framework that can accommodate products as they launch.

Each product should eventually be able to have:

product name

product image

concise description

problem it solves

product story

specifications

price

availability

purchase CTA

Kickstarter/preorder CTA where appropriate

The visual presentation should resemble a high-end product design company rather than a conventional online store.

Products should have substantial whitespace.

Use large product imagery.

Keep product pages extremely clean.

Do not create fake products or fake prices.

Use placeholder states where necessary.

The first products will be small household/personal inventions.

The product section should be capable of evolving from:

COMING SOON

to:

KICKSTARTER

to:

AVAILABLE NOW

without requiring redesign.

12. JOIN

JOIN should be about participation.

Create two major pathways:

STAY CONNECTED

Email newsletter signup.

The signup should feel intentional rather than like a generic marketing popup.

Possible language:

JOIN THE LAB

Follow what we're building.

Allow the user to enter their email.

Do not add excessive fields.

SUPPORT THE VISION

Create a prominent space for supporting SKYE's larger vision financially.

Potentially this will eventually include a DONATE button.

IMPORTANT:

Do not imply that SKYE_LABS is currently a registered nonprofit or that donations are currently tax-deductible.

The nonprofit/charitable structure is still being developed.

For now, design the interface so a future legitimate donation system can be integrated cleanly.

Possible temporary state:

SUPPORT THE VISION

Help us build what comes next.

DONATE — COMING SOON

The interface should make it easy to replace this with an actual donation system later.

13. WHY WE NEED OTHERS

Within VISION or JOIN, include a section explaining that SKYE_LABS cannot build the entire vision alone.

The company will eventually need:

financial supporters

investors where appropriate

manufacturers

engineers

software developers

designers

marketers

community organizers

advisors

strategic partners

nonprofit/community partners

people who simply believe in the mission

This should NOT feel like a generic “we're hiring!” page.

The idea is broader:

BUILD WITH US.

Some people may contribute money.

Some may contribute expertise.

Some may contribute relationships.

Some may become customers.

Some may simply follow the work.

The website should communicate that building SKYE is intended to be participatory.

14. JOIN / FUTURE OPPORTUNITIES

Create room for future categories such as:

WORK WITH US

PARTNER WITH US

SUPPORT US

JOIN THE TEAM

But don't make all of these active unless there is actual functionality behind them.

The architecture should allow them to be activated later.

15. COPY PHILOSOPHY

The writing should feel:

intelligent

direct

human

confident

optimistic

thoughtful

concise

occasionally provocative

Avoid corporate jargon.

Avoid phrases like:

“revolutionizing the ecosystem”

“leveraging innovative solutions”

“disrupting industries”

“empowering stakeholders”

“cutting-edge solutions”

Do not sound like a venture capital pitch deck.

SKYE_LABS should sound like a person with a serious idea who intends to build it.

The company should have conviction without pretending to already have everything figured out.

16. BRAND VOICE

SKYE_LABS should communicate a belief that difficult things are worth attempting.

The phrase:

NEVER IMPOSSIBLE.

should feel like a philosophy rather than a marketing gimmick.

The company is ambitious, but should remain grounded.

The tone should suggest:

“This may be difficult. We're building it anyway.”

17. IMAGERY

Use imagery sparingly.

When imagery is used, favor:

architectural photography

industrial design

material studies

close-ups of objects

manufacturing

people in real environments

NYC

community spaces

infrastructure

experimental technology

abstract black-and-white textures

All imagery should be treated monochromatically.

Avoid generic corporate stock photography.

Avoid photographs of smiling business teams shaking hands.

Avoid cheesy “innovation” imagery.

Avoid excessive imagery if typography and whitespace communicate the idea better.

18. MOTION

Animation should be subtle and purposeful.

Use:

gentle fades

controlled transitions

subtle movement

smooth scrolling

restrained hover states

elegant page transitions where appropriate

Do NOT use:

bouncing elements

excessive parallax

flashy transitions

spinning objects

constant animation

animated gradients

Motion should make the website feel alive without distracting from the content.

19. RESPONSIVE DESIGN

The website must work beautifully on:

desktop

laptop

tablet

mobile

On mobile, preserve the visual hierarchy and typography.

Do not simply compress desktop layouts.

The site should feel intentionally designed for mobile.

Navigation should become a minimal mobile menu.

20. ACCESSIBILITY

Maintain strong black/white contrast.

Use semantic HTML.

Provide appropriate alt text for images.

Ensure buttons and navigation are keyboard accessible.

Maintain readable body text sizes.

Do not sacrifice accessibility for aesthetics.

21. TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE

Build the site so that content is easy to update.

Important content such as:

mission

vision

phase descriptions

product descriptions

product status

newsletter text

future initiatives

should not be deeply hardcoded into complicated components.

Use a clean component structure.

Create reusable components for:

Navigation

Footer

Section headers

Product cards

Phase sections

CTA blocks

Newsletter signup

Support/Donate CTA

Page transitions

Typography hierarchy

The design system should make future pages easy to create.

22. HOMEPAGE INFORMATION HIERARCHY

The homepage should roughly follow this conceptual flow:

SKYE_LABS

NEVER IMPOSSIBLE.

↓

Short introduction to the company

↓

VISION

A concise introduction to the larger vision.

CTA: EXPLORE THE VISION

↓

PRODUCTS

Introduce the current/coming products.

CTA: VIEW PRODUCTS

↓

JOIN

Invite people to follow, participate, support, or eventually work with SKYE.

CTA: JOIN US

↓

Minimal footer.

23. FOOTER

Keep the footer extremely clean.

Include:

SKYE_LABS

BY SKYE_GROUP

New York City

Then minimal links such as:

VISION
PRODUCTS
JOIN
CONTACT
INSTAGRAM / YOUTUBE / FACEBOOK where applicable

Do not clutter the footer.

24. IMPORTANT BUSINESS HONESTY

The company is currently in an early development stage.

Do not fabricate:

customers

sales

investors

employees

partnerships

funding

product availability

nonprofit status

charitable deductions

app availability

manufacturing relationships

testimonials

statistics

awards

Use placeholders or “coming soon” states where necessary.

The website should feel ambitious but truthful.

25. THE CENTRAL DESIGN IDEA

The website should communicate a simple progression:

WE SEE A PROBLEM.

↓

WE DESIGN SOMETHING.

↓

PEOPLE USE IT.

↓

THE VALUE CREATED FUNDS WHAT COMES NEXT.

↓

THE COMPANY GROWS.

↓

THE CAPACITY TO HELP OTHER PEOPLE GROWS.

This progression is central to the SKYE_LABS story.

Physical products are not disconnected side projects.

They are intended to become the economic foundation for larger technological and community initiatives.

26. FINAL DESIGN TEST

Before considering the website complete, ask:

If someone sees the homepage for ten seconds, do they understand that SKYE_LABS is:

A PRODUCT DESIGN COMPANY

with a larger ambition to use:

PRODUCTS → TECHNOLOGY → COMMUNITY

to address real human problems?

If someone spends five minutes on the site, do they understand:

WHAT WE MAKE

WHY WE MAKE IT

WHERE WE ARE GOING

and

HOW THEY CAN PARTICIPATE?

If yes, the design is successful.

The final site should feel like the beginning of something much larger—not like a finished corporation pretending to be bigger than it is.

SKYE_LABS

NEVER IMPOSSIBLE.
